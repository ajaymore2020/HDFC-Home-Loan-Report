Conclusion
The 10-year HDFC Bank home loan report shows a strong and expanding loan portfolio with high customer participation and consistent financial activity.

Key Insights
The bank served 10,000 customers over the 10-year period.
Total loan disbursement reached ₹64M, while ₹34M has already been repaid, indicating healthy repayment progress.
A very large majority of loans are marked as “Not Clear” (active loans), meaning most customers are still in the repayment cycle.
The loan status overview shows approximately 95% active loans, with only a small percentage closed or defaulted, reflecting good portfolio stability.
Loan growth fluctuated across months but generally maintained a positive trend, with recovery toward the end of the year.
